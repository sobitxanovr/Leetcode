## 5. Seedance 2.0 prompts (one 10-second clip per block, five hard-cut shots each)

> Flat 2D vector explainer animation, bold saturated palette of teal, coral, mustard and white, thick clean outlines, rounded simple characters, soft drop shadows, subtle paper grain. Smooth motion from the very first frame. Characters only emote and gesture, they do NOT talk. No text, no letters, no logos anywhere in frame. Five hard cuts, each shot about two seconds, varied shot sizes and angles.

**Block 3 — guest not host**
